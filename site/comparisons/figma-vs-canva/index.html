Quick verdict

Figma for interface design; Canva for rapid content creation

Figma is built around collaborative interface and product design. Canva is built around approachable template-driven content creation. They overlap at the edges, but their strongest workflows solve different problems.

Decision guide

Which one should you choose?

Choose Figma

If you design interfaces, prototypes, design systems or collaborate closely with product and development teams.

Choose Canva

If you need to create branded posts, presentations, flyers and marketing assets quickly from templates.
