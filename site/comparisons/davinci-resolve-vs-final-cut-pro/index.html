Quick verdict

Resolve for all-in-one finishing; Final Cut for streamlined Mac editing

DaVinci Resolve is the stronger all-in-one finishing environment across edit, color, VFX and audio. Final Cut Pro is an excellent choice for Mac editors who value speed, Apple integration and its distinctive Magnetic Timeline.

Side by side

Where each tool wins

Use this as a workflow decision, not a popularity contest.

DaVinci Resolve

Strengths

  • Industry-leading color workflow
  • Fusion VFX and Fairlight audio included
  • Powerful free version
  • Cross-platform

Tradeoffs

  • Huge toolset can feel dense
  • Some advanced features require Studio

Final Cut Pro

Strengths

  • Fast Apple-silicon performance
  • Magnetic Timeline
  • Strong Apple ecosystem integration
  • One-time Mac purchase available

Tradeoffs

  • Mac-only desktop workflow
  • Magnetic Timeline can require adjustment

Decision guide

Which one should you choose?

Choose DaVinci Resolve

If you want editing, grading, VFX and audio finishing under one roof or collaborate in color-heavy post workflows.

Choose Final Cut Pro

If you edit on Mac, prioritize speed and responsiveness, and enjoy a trackless Magnetic Timeline approach.
