Quick verdict

Workflow dependent

ChatGPT is a broad creative and multimodal workspace with a large tool surface. Claude is especially attractive for long-form writing, reasoning and document-heavy workflows. The better choice depends on the work you do most.

Decision guide

Which one should you choose?

Choose ChatGPT

If you want one general creative AI workspace spanning images, research, coding, brainstorming and everyday tasks.

Choose Claude

If your work leans heavily toward long documents, careful writing, analysis and code.
